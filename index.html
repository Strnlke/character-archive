Storage & recovery status

The recovery snapshot exists only while this tab is open; browser reload or closing destroys it. Imported manuscript copies are excluded from RAM recovery; they remain in regular writer ZIP backups. Keep your original manuscripts.

Backup reminder preferences and last download initiation are stored in this browser only. They do not confirm that a downloaded ZIP was copied to another device.

Storage recovery

Emergency Save copies available files from this tab's memory into a NEW recovery folder. It does not overwrite or reconnect the original library. If a full snapshot is unavailable, only the current writer's recoverable files are copied. Imported manuscript copies and World Maps source images are never included in RAM recovery; re-import/re-link originals or use a regular writer ZIP backup.

Do not reload the browser until the recovery is saved somewhere safe. An incomplete recovery is not a backup.

Backup & restore

Writer ZIPs include all books, character JSON, pictures, timelines, trees, and revision files. They stay on your computer.

Review writer backup

Restoration creates a NEW writer profile; it never overwrites your existing writer or character files. Confirm only after checking the details.

Character Revision History

Saved snapshots from earlier editing sessions. Restoring a revision first backs up your current profile.
